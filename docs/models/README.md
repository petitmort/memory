`src/assets/sleeping-tabby.glb` is an adaptation for this project. The surrounding tabletop and hanging original tail were removed. A shorter, curved and tapered tail was reconstructed using the photographed coat. Geometry was conservatively reduced, normals regenerated, and the photographic texture resized to 1024 × 1024 JPEG. The hind legs were folded inward, the head was turned in and lowered toward the paws with a smooth neck blend, and the tail was fitted closely to the flank. Seven stray pale scan triangles beneath the chin were removed. The final curled pose spans about 43 × 32 × 11 cm with its support at Y=0. It is a static scan; the application applies a subtle, grounded breathing deformation.

The result has 20,207 triangles, one mesh, one PBR material and one embedded texture, totaling 760,972 bytes. Vite emits a content-hashed GLB filename so pose updates load a new asset. The adapted GLB also includes the attribution and modifications in `asset.extras`. Visible attribution is available from the room's “猫咪模型鸣谢” link to [the credits page](../credits.html).
