# Sleeping tabby cat

- **Original:** [Sleeping Cat On The Table - 3D Scan](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sleeping-cat-on-the-table-3d-scan-d9c2ee02107a456eac00356bb43ea4bd)

- **Source archive:** [original glTF, geometry and textures](https://github.com/larissaloza/Stelar-Sanctum-Bad/tree/main/Biblioteca/Modelos/sleeping_cat_on_the_table_-_3d_scan). The original glTF's `asset.extras` identifies the author, title, Sketchfab source and CC BY 4.0 license.

`src/assets/sleeping-tabby.glb` is an adaptation for this project. The surrounding tabletop and hanging original tail were removed. A shorter, curved and tapered tail was reconstructed using the photographed coat. Geometry was conservatively reduced, normals regenerated, and the photographic texture resized to 1024 × 1024 JPEG. The hind legs were folded inward, the head was turned in and lowered toward the paws with a smooth neck blend, and the tail was fitted closely to the flank. Seven stray pale scan triangles beneath the chin were removed. The final curled pose spans about 43 × 32 × 11 cm with its support at Y=0.

The GLB remains a static scan without an embedded animation skeleton. At load time, `src/cat-petting.ts` derives two local GPU morph targets from that scan: a small head-and-neck lift/nuzzle and an ear flick. Their normals are recomputed with the scan's duplicate UV vertices welded for smooth shading. The torso, paws and support plane have zero position displacement; the original photographic UVs and texture remain attached to the moving anatomy. `src/sleeping-cat.ts` retains subtle grounded breathing and plays a short petting reaction before returning the morph weights exactly to zero. Repeated input during the reaction or cooldown does not queue another pose. The normal reaction lasts about 2.15 seconds; reduced-motion mode uses about 0.65 seconds with smaller movement.

The result has 20,207 triangles, one mesh, one PBR material and one embedded texture, totaling 760,972 bytes. Runtime morph positions and normals add 557,184 bytes of buffers (approximately 544 KiB); they add no triangles, meshes, photographed material textures or GLB download bytes. Targets are prepared before the model's ready promise resolves, so the room's shader compilation and GPU warmup include them. Vite emits a content-hashed GLB filename so static pose updates load a new asset. The adapted GLB also includes the static attribution and modifications in `asset.extras`. Runtime animation modifications are documented here. Visible attribution for the model and the separate purr-and-meow recording is available from the room's “猫咪素材鸣谢” link to [the credits page](../credits.html); sound provenance is documented in [the audio notes](../audio/README.md).
