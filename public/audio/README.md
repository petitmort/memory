# Cat purring

`src/assets/cat-purr.mp3` is a short adaptation of **“Cat Purr” (#0436)** by **Joseph Sardin**, BigSoundBank:

- Original recording: <https://bigsoundbank.com/detail-0436-cat-purr.html>
- License: **CC0 / public-domain equivalent**, as identified in the source mirror's [pinned asset credits](https://github.com/druboni/find-the-cat/blob/0ee6eb153807ebff3c50cdff5778830b74e36b5a/CREDITS.md).
- CC0 legal text: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Source WAV mirror: [purr_found.wav at the same pinned commit](https://github.com/druboni/find-the-cat/blob/0ee6eb153807ebff3c50cdff5778830b74e36b5a/assets/audio/purr_found.wav). The mirror's MIT license applies to its game source, not to the recording; its credits explicitly identify this recording separately as CC0.

For this site, the 0.60–3.20 second section was converted to mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at 80 kb/s, lightly filtered below 22 Hz and above 1.8 kHz, lowered by 2 dB, and given 100 ms / 400 ms fades. The shipped file is 26,689 bytes, approximately 2.6 seconds. Vite emits a content-hashed filename; the original ten-second WAV is not shipped. No new vocalization or synthetic meow is mixed into the source recording.

The original WAV and pinned credit evidence are retained in the local authoring workspace at `.work/cat-audio/`. No attribution obligation is added by this adaptation; the original author and provenance are retained here voluntarily.
